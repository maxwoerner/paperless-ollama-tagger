# Rules

## General
- Titles: short and descriptive, in the document's language, no file extension.
  Style: "<Sender> – <Subject>", e.g. "Telekom – Rechnung Oktober 2026".

## Health insurance (examples — replace with your own)
- Invoices and reimbursement notices for Anna's private health insurance get the tag `pkv-anna`.
- Doctor invoices ("Arztrechnung") get the document type `Rechnung`; fill `Rechnungsbetrag` with the total amount due.
